## What it means to me…

One of my main focuses is information literacy. Information literacy is more important than ever in the age of AI. There are many ways that AI can benefit the acquisition of information but it can also generate fake citations or fail to meet existing standards in producing novel work. It is important that the library give access to technological tools and also provide the means to evaluate the right place to use these tools and how to assess their effectiveness.

## How I have demonstrated this…

As part of my MSLIS I got a CAS in IT Management. The focus of this CAS is not only on building and understanding technological systems but also understanding how to do effective digital transformation and empower both users and coworkers to improve existing infrastructure and adopt new technological paradigms. As part of this I did work designing systems to manage O(millions) of users and also evaluated existing and proposed AI systems and described how these learnings could be applied within a public library system to manage meeting room booking. As part of my coursework I also learned how to help patrons to navigate online systems both for metadata and reference. This helped to inform my proposal at the Wayland Free Library for improving user’s ability to use the website to search for information and giving access to tools for joining library programs online via teleconferencing software.

## What I plan to do in the future…

In the future I will continue at my current job working as a Senior Cloud Database Engineer which keeps me up to date with cutting edge tools including AI and also continue in my part time work as a library clerk at the Wayland Library where we are making the improvements proposed in the existing project proposal and are also working to digitize historical newspapers and books and are collaborating with other libraries to create online collections using the Omeka archival tools. Information literacy will become more important and not less important as AI becomes more and more a part of modern life and I will continue to learn more and make sure to empower users so they get the benefits and are not harmed by ignorance.
