## What it means to me…

In my philosophy I said “Since the Enlightenment the goals of libraries have changed, and now they embody the Enlightenment values of intellectual freedom, access and democracy… need to be able to move quickly in order to preserve those values and continue to be a resource not only for the wealthy and powerful but also for people who can use them to improve their lives and improve our world by pushing the boundaries of what is possible.” I would say this is the most important part of my personal philosophy to me. In the last fifty years we have had tremendous advances in technology, and the benefit of those technologies has been incredibly unequal. A library is a place that treats everyone equally and is dedicated to reducing inequity and giving access to information for everyone equally.

## How I have demonstrated this…

In addition to my coursework on cultural competency and information management I have also pursued a CAS in IT management. As part of this work I created multiple technical systems including a fully functional library event management database for scheduling time to use rooms. As part of my work at the Wayland Free Library I also built an Omeka site to integrate data from the academic and public libraries in the SCRLC organization. I also created a proposal with the library director to update the library web page to provide additional language options for immigrants and in my coursework I also gained knowledge on how to provide detailed descriptions in online catalogues to meet the needs of patrons.

## What I plan to do in the future…

I will continue to learn more about technology including things like AI that are having major impacts in libraries at this time. It is not only the case that AI impacts the reliability of information and that people have to be informed about how to verify what information they get from AI but also that the availability of AI tools is not equal. In the same way that accessibility for the internet and to online databases is important now in the future access to AI tools will also be important.
